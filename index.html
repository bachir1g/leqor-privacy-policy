LEQOR DZ Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

LEQOR DZ is an educational platform designed to connect students, teachers, course providers, and educational institutions.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, LEQOR DZ may collect and process:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account and user identifiers
  • Wilaya and Commune information
  • Profile photo
  • Teacher or institution verification documents
  • Payment status, payment method, and purchase history
  • Payment proof images or documents
  • Messages sent through in-app chat
  • Bookings and appointments
  • Reviews and ratings
  • Institution posts
  • Digital library activity

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide educational services
  • Connect students with teachers and institutions
  • Manage lessons, bookings, and appointments
  • Process and verify payments
  • Verify teachers and educational institutions
  • Provide in-app communication and messaging
  • Send service-related notifications
  • Provide and manage digital educational resources
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse
  • Operate, maintain, and improve LEQOR DZ

3. Third-Party Services

LEQOR DZ uses third-party infrastructure and service providers, including Supabase and Firebase, to provide certain features such as authentication, databases, file storage, messaging, notifications, and administrative logging.

These providers may process information on behalf of LEQOR DZ as necessary to provide their services.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not share personal information with third parties for their independent advertising purposes.

Information may be processed by service providers supporting LEQOR DZ, or disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the service, its users, or our legal rights.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted connections and access controls.

However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with applicable legal or accounting obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain necessary records.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type and purpose of the information.

7. Account and Data Deletion

Users can request deletion of their LEQOR DZ account through the account deletion feature available in the application.

When an account deletion request is completed, associated account data and supported stored files are deleted.

Certain booking, financial, security, or audit records may be retained when necessary for legitimate legal, financial, security, or audit purposes.

8. Location Information

LEQOR DZ may collect Wilaya and Commune information provided by users for educational and service-related purposes.

LEQOR DZ does not require continuous GPS location tracking to provide its core services.

9. Children's Privacy

LEQOR DZ is not specifically directed to children under 13. Users and guardians should use the service in accordance with applicable age requirements and local law.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
